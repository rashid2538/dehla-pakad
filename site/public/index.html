Game Features

Real-Time Multiplayer

Play with friends anywhere using room codes. Firestore real-time sync ensures instant card plays and trick resolution across all devices.

Smart Bot Players

Can't find four humans? Add AI bots with medium-difficulty strategy — they protect Tens, manage trump, and play as a team.

Court & Poopy Victories

Win by collecting all four Tens or holding the majority. Earn the prestigious Court Victory or the dreaded Poopy Victory!

How to Play

Create a Room

Sign in with Google, create a room, and share the 6-letter code with your friends.

Fill the Table

Wait for 4 players to join, or add bots to fill empty seats. Partners sit across from each other.

Play Cards

Follow suit, set trump when void, and strategize with your partner to capture those precious Tens.

Grab the Tens!

Collect all four Tens for an instant win, or hold the majority after 13 tricks.

Quick Rules

  • 4 players in 2 teams — partners sit opposite (seats 1 & 3 vs 2 & 4)
  • Standard 52-card deck, all cards dealt (13 each)
  • Must follow the led suit — play any card if void in that suit
  • First void play sets trump for the rest of the game
  • Highest card of led suit wins the trick (unless trumped)
  • All 4 Tens to one team = immediate victory
  • After 13 tricks: more Tens wins; if tied 2-2, more tricks wins
